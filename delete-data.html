Delete your Nightcap data

Nightcap: Bedtime App Blocker, by Harshit Creative Studio

On your phone — one tap

Open Nightcap → My Account → Delete all my data, and confirm. This erases everything Nightcap stored on your phone at once: your schedule, the apps you chose, your unlock settings and your night history. Uninstalling Nightcap does the same.

Your Nightcap Pro record

To know whether you have Nightcap Pro, Nightcap uses RevenueCat, which holds a random ID made for your phone and your purchase history — never your name, email or anything about your nights. To have that record deleted, email harshitcreativestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". If you can, include the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.) so we can find the right record.

What is deleted, and when

Data on your phone: deleted immediately when you tap Delete all my data. Your RevenueCat record (the random ID and purchase history): deleted within 30 days of your email.

What is kept

Google Play keeps its own record of your purchase for billing, refunds and the law, under Google’s privacy policy — we cannot delete it. A copy in your phone’s own backup, if you have backups switched on, is in your Google account; delete it there (Google Drive → Backups). Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. To stop paying, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
