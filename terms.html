What Nightcap does

Nightcap helps you keep chosen apps out of reach during the hours you set. It is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and it does not measure or treat sleep.

What it can’t promise

Pausing apps depends on the permissions you grant, and it can stop working if a permission is turned off, if the phone’s battery settings close Nightcap, or if the app is uninstalled. When Nightcap knows protection isn’t working, it tells you on Home.
