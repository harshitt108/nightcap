Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Nightcap is built so that we never have your data to begin with. This policy explains what the app uses on your phone and why.

No account

Nightcap never asks for your name, email or phone number, and there is no sign-in. We do not know who you are.

What stays on your phone

Your sleep schedule, the apps you chose to pause, your wait and break settings, and a record of each night — how often a paused app was opened, what you chose, and for how long a break was used. This is what Insights is built from. It is stored on this device, and nowhere else Nightcap controls (see Phone backups).

What we collect

Nothing. Nightcap has no server, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. None of your data is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Usage Access lets Nightcap see which app is on screen, so it can pause the ones you chose during your sleep hours. Display over other apps lets it show the pause screen on top of them. It also reads the list of apps on your phone so you can pick from it. While Nightcap is set up it keeps one quiet notification showing, all day — Android requires one for the service that watches for your bedtime. None of this leaves your phone.

Phone backups

If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, Android may include Nightcap’s settings and night history in that backup, and restore them onto a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account — we never see it, and it is managed from your Google account’s backup settings.

Purchases

If you buy Nightcap Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

My Account → Delete all my data erases everything Nightcap stored on this phone, and uninstalling the app does the same. Neither can reach a copy in your phone’s own backup (see Phone backups); that is deleted from your Google account.

Children

Nightcap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone — including them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. If Nightcap ever starts sending data anywhere, this policy will say so first.
