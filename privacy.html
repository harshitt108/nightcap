Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Nightcap is built so that we never have your data to begin with. This policy explains what the app uses on your phone and why.

No account

Nightcap never asks for your name, email or phone number, and there is no sign-in. We do not know who you are.

What stays on your phone

Your sleep schedule, the apps you chose to pause, your wait and break settings, and a record of each night — how often a paused app was opened, what you chose, and for how long a break was used. This is what Insights is built from. It is stored on this device, and nowhere else Nightcap controls (see Phone backups).

What we collect

Your schedule, the apps you chose and your night history are never sent anywhere. Nightcap has no server of its own, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. The one thing that leaves your phone is what is needed to know whether you have Nightcap Pro — see Purchases.

Permissions

Usage Access lets Nightcap see which app is on screen, so it can pause the ones you chose during your sleep hours. Display over other apps lets it show the pause screen on top of them. It also reads the list of apps on your phone so you can pick from it. While Nightcap is set up it keeps one quiet notification showing, all day — Android requires one for the service that watches for your bedtime. None of this leaves your phone.

Phone backups

If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, Android may include Nightcap’s settings and night history in that backup, and restore them onto a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account — we never see it, and it is managed from your Google account’s backup settings.

Purchases

Nightcap Pro is paid for through Google Play or the App Store, under their own privacy policies — we never see your card details. To know whether you have Pro, Nightcap uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that works on our behalf: it receives a random ID the app makes for this phone, your purchase history, and basic technical details such as the app version, the device model and your country. It never receives your name, your email, or anything about your nights. This happens for everyone who opens the app, so it can tell a free user from a Pro one; RevenueCat uses it only to check purchases, under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

My Account → Delete all my data erases everything Nightcap stored on this phone, and uninstalling the app does the same. Neither can reach a copy in your phone’s own backup (see Phone backups); that is deleted from your Google account. Purchase records are kept by Google Play for billing; to have the RevenueCat record deleted, write to harshitcreativestudio@gmail.com.
